Property transaction costs

Estimate the one-off costs of buying real estate in Bulgaria on top of the price: notary fee, local transfer tax, and the Registry Agency entry fee.

Notary fee uses the official proportional tariff (Art. 8, Тарифа за нотариалните такси) computed in BGN and converted at 1.95583, capped at 6000 BGN (≈ €3,067.75). Transfer tax is municipal (0.1–3%; e.g. ~3% in Sofia). Costs are usually charged on the higher of price or tax assessment. Indicative only — verify the current tariff, your municipality's rate, and any VAT on new builds.
