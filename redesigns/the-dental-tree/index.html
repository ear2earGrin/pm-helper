Frequently asked questions.

A simple FAQ block for visitors before they call or book an appointment.

Why should I visit the dentist regularly?

Regular checkups help prevent problems before they become painful or more complex. Prevention keeps teeth and gums healthier and makes treatment simpler.

What can I do if I feel fear before the appointment?

The clinic focuses on calm explanation, patience and a relaxed atmosphere, so every step is clear and the patient feels safe.

Do you offer aesthetic dentistry and whitening?

Yes. The clinic includes whitening and aesthetic dentistry services with attention to a natural, bright result.
